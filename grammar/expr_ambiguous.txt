# Ambiguous expression grammar made deterministic by precedence declarations.
# Same language as grammar/expr_layered.txt: binary + - * /, unary minus,
# parentheses, identifiers and numbers. Used for the ambiguous-vs-layered
# experiment (fewer states and no unit reductions, but every operator
# conflict is resolved by %left / %right).
%left  + -
%left  * /
%right UMINUS
E -> E + E | E - E | E * E | E / E
E -> - E %prec UMINUS
E -> ( E ) | id | num
